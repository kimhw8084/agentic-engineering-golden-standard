# Employee workflows and staged adoption

This template contains no employee application. Consuming products must deliver a minimum
lovable end-to-end workflow before claiming adoption. Ordinary employees need no terminal,
Git, prompt editing or API credentials.

Manufacturing System Intelligence journey: sign in → choose authorized revision → ask how
one parameter flows → inspect source/validator citations and unknowns → save a reviewable
answer → give feedback or cancel. Show scope, freshness, progress and recovery. Source links
resolve under employee permissions. Loading/empty/denied/stale/unavailable/timeout/canceled
states need understandable actions and keyboard-accessible controls. No equipment action.

Acceptance: observed employee completes the journey without developer help, including a
recoverable source failure. Predeclare quality/grounding/latency targets with the sponsor
from a baseline. Test feedback, cancel, session expiry and retry through UI and adapters.
Outages must not appear as absence. Assign support, retention and rollback ownership.

| Stage | Entry / owner | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Named sponsor and engineering/source/security owners | Bounded problem, authorized corpus, baseline plan, hypothesis and stop criteria |
| Narrow pilot | Read-only scope; opt-in cohort | No-terminal journey, safety gates, observed task outcomes/failures |
| Measured benefit | Frozen evaluation and manual/assisted comparison | Net minutes after review/rework/maintenance, quality and uncertainty; sponsor decision |
| Team rollout | Operator, runbook, canary and rollback | Sustained usage/benefit, incidents, ACL/freshness and feedback regressions |
| Cross-group reuse | Second team/source owner accepts contract | Independent second-team outcome, classification, compatibility and ownership |

Promote by evidence, not date. Regress when safety/value fails. Measurement formula stays
in `IMPACT_AND_EXECUTIVE_RECOGNITION.md`; release gates in `DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md`.
