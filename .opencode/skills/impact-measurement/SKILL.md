# Impact Measurement

## Procedure
Define a concrete repetitive workflow, volume and eligibility. Collect actual active manual and assisted labor, review and rework; use matched/crossover measurement where feasible. Compute net hours, uncertainty and excluded cases. Include defects, audit quality, adoption and platform maintenance labor. Separate measured results from extrapolation and sponsors' expectations. Use `templates/EXPERIMENT.md` and `docs/IMPACT_AND_EXECUTIVE_RECOGNITION.md`. Never fabricate executive-award probability.
