# Interview Defense

## Procedure
Choose a specific shipped capability and supporting evidence. Prepare 90-second, 5-minute and 30-minute explanations: why problem mattered, relevant data, architecture, alternatives, operational failures, tests, quantitative results, user's independent ownership. Probe algorithmic complexity, concurrency, security, data modeling, quality measurement and leadership influence. Challenge weak and invented claims. Generate portable synthetic exercise and approved anonymized story only; never export company IP.
